Privacy Policy for MYVI

Last updated: November 2026

At MYVI, we deeply respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

MYVI operates strictly on a local-first principle. All your personal data, vision boards, journal entries, progress images, and daily notes are stored locally on your device using secure local storage (AsyncStorage). We do not collect, transmit, store, or process your personal data on any external servers or cloud databases.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing

Because all data remains exclusively on your physical device, no data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by third parties, advertisers, or tracking services.

3. Media and Photos

If you choose to upload images to your vision boards or journal entries, these images are stored locally on your device's file system. We do not have access to your photo library or stored images.

4. App Permissions

MYVI may request permissions for local notifications to send you daily vision check-ins. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve external messaging servers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy or this application, please contact us via email at: lautorni@outlook.de
