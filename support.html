Turn intentions into progress.

A practical guide to creating visions, recording the journey, and celebrating what you accomplish.

What you can do in MyVi

Ongoing

Keep active visions visible and open a vision card to see its details.

Achieved

Keep a visual record of completed visions, including your proof photo and reflection.

Stats.

Review achievement rate, time to achievement, views, and vision progress.

All

Browse a date-organized timeline of journal entries, vision comments, and achievements.

Getting started

  1. 1

    Create a vision

    On the Ongoing tab, tap + Add Vision. Add a title, an optional description, and a photo from your camera or gallery. Tap Make Your Vision Happen to save it.

  2. 2

    Track your progress

    Open a vision card to review it. Add notes or comments as you make progress; comments can include images and can be edited later.

  3. 3

    Edit a vision

    Open the vision card and choose the edit option. You can update its title, description, or image without losing its existing progress.

  4. 4

    Complete a vision

    When your goal is complete, choose the completion action, upload a proof photo, and optionally describe the accomplishment. The vision then moves to Achieved.

Stories and daily reflection

Tap the MyVi icon in the header when a story is available. Stories show your active visions one at a time, with an automatic six-second progress cycle.

Tap the sides of the screen to move backward or forward, or pause while reading. Open comments to read the conversation, add a comment, or edit one of your existing comments.

Each active vision becomes available as a story again after 24 hours. Achieved visions are not included in the story cycle.

Journal

Open the All tab and tap + Add Journal to write a title and reflection. You can attach a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera.

Choose a mood color while writing from the journal timeline. Tap a journal entry twice to edit it. Use the timeline filters to view all entries, only diary entries, or only vision activity.

Settings, reminders, and your data

From the settings panel, you can enable daily vision reminders and choose the reminder time. If reminders do not appear, check that MyVi has permission to send notifications in your device settings.

The settings panel also includes Download Your Data. Use this to export or import your MyVi data and preserve your visions, images, comments, and journal entries.

The photobook option is currently marked as coming soon.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my story icon not active?

Stories are created from active visions that have not been viewed in the last 24 hours. Create an ongoing vision or wait until the cooldown has ended.

Why can’t I complete a vision?

A completion photo is required. Grant camera or photo-library permission, select an image, and try again.

Can I remove an image before saving?

Yes. Tap the image or its remove control while editing a vision or journal entry, then save your changes.

Where is my content stored?

MyVi keeps visions and journal entries on the device so they remain available when you return to the app. Use the data controls in Settings to create a portable backup.
